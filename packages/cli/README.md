# @luzzle/cli 💻

A terminal companion for the human side of Luzzle. 

## What it's for 🛠️

* **Manual Management**: Quickly add, edit, or validate pieces without leaving the terminal.
* **Schema Support**: Uses your archive's schemas to provide smart prompts for new records.
* **Sanity Checks**: Validates your entire archive to ensure every piece is in the right place and follows its schema.
