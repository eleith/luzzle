Makes four modest bowls. The beans do the thickening, so there is no need for
cream or a blender.

Start with half the lemon juice and taste before adding the rest. On the second
day the soup needs a splash of water and another squeeze of lemon to wake it up.

A handful of chopped greens would fit nicely near the end of cooking.
