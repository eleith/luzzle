  - item: olive oil

instructions:
  - text: Heat the oven to 220°C. Toss the tomatoes, oil and garlic in a roasting dish and season with salt.
  - text: Roast for 25 minutes, until the tomatoes soften and their edges begin to darken.
  - text: Meanwhile, cook the pasta in salted water. Reserve a mug of cooking water before draining.
  - text: Mash the tomatoes and garlic with a fork. Toss with the pasta, loosening the sauce with a splash of cooking water.
  - text: Fold in the basil and serve with another drizzle of olive oil.

Enough for two hungry people, or three with a salad.

The first time I made this, I drained the pasta too thoroughly. Keeping some
starchy water nearby makes the sauce cling instead of collecting underneath.

Next time: add a spoonful of capers to the roasting dish and save a portion for
lunch.
