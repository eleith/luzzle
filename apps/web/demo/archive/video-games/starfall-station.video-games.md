A fictional exploration game with no timer and very few instructions. Each
repaired room makes the station a little warmer and opens a new way through.

## What worked

- The map remembers the paths I actually walked.
- Small environmental details explain what happened without stopping play.
- Returning to the first room after restoring power changes its meaning.

I spent just over four hours here. The optional observatory puzzle was worth
coming back for after the credits.
