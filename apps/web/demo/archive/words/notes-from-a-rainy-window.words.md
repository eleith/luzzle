## Before the rain

The street was unusually quiet. Someone had left a blue umbrella open on a
balcony, as if it were waiting for instructions.

## During

- Water found the same crack in the windowsill it found last winter.
- The tree across the road became several different greens.
- A delivery rider stopped beneath the awning and ate an orange.

> Staying in one place is sometimes enough to notice a journey.

## After

I meant to make a list of things to finish. Instead, I wrote down three things
I would otherwise have forgotten. The unfinished work was still there when the
sun came back, but the afternoon no longer felt empty.
