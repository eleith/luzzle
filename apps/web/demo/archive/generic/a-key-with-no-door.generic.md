---
title: A Key with No Door
summary: A brass key from a tin of spare buttons, kept for its shape long after anyone remembered the lock.
date_added: 2026-10-31
keywords: keepsakes, objects, family, keeping things
---
The label fell off years ago. Nobody in the family can agree whether the key
belonged to a desk, a garden gate or the cupboard under the stairs.

It has a pleasing weight and a small crescent worn into one edge. I keep it on
the shelf beside the tram ticket, which is probably not a classification system
but is good enough for now.

> An object can lose its purpose without losing its story.
