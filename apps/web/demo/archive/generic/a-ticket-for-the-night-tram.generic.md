Some things are worth keeping before I know which collection they belong in.
This ticket has a faded date, a blue stripe and a corner folded by too many
trips through a coat pocket.

The tram stopped short of the river, so we walked the rest of the way. There
was a bakery still open near the bridge.

- Keep the story beside the object.
- Leave room to add a photograph later.
- Do not invent a special category just to save one good memory.
