A fictional film made of ferry crossings, short conversations and meals that
last a little longer than expected. There is very little urgency, which is
exactly what the story needs.

The framing keeps finding rectangles inside the landscape: envelopes, cabin
windows, the bright doorway of a shop at closing time.

> Not every letter needs an answer. Some only need to arrive.

A good companion to a quiet afternoon. I wanted another ten minutes with the
family who run the smallest post office on the route.
