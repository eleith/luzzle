A fictional, mostly nocturnal mystery that makes a railway timetable feel like
a collection of promises.

The best scenes barely move: a kettle coming to the boil, a signal changing in
the distance, two people listening for the same train. The warm station windows
against the blue mountains do almost as much storytelling as the dialogue.

I liked that the ending answers the practical question and leaves the emotional
one open. Watch without looking up the plot first.
