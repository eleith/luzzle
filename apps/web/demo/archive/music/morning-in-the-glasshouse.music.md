A fictional song that leaves the little sounds in: a chair moving, a breath
before the verse, something tapping gently against the glass.

The guitar is close and dry, but the backing voices seem to come from the far
end of the room. It is a lovely contrast to the late-night bass of
*After the Last Tram*.

Play this one while making breakfast, then start it again when the room is quiet.
