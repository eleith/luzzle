A fictional track for an imaginary walk home after the city has gone quiet.

I like the way the bass arrives before the drums. The little keyboard figure
returns near the end, but a few notes have moved; it feels familiar without
quite landing in the same place.

**Best moment:** the pause just before the final chorus. Listen with headphones
rather than leaving it in the background.
