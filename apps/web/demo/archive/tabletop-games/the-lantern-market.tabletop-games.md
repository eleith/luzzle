A fictional card game for an evening around the kitchen table. Every round,
one player knows which visitor is coming, but needs help gathering the right
collection of goods.

Our four-player game took about 45 minutes, including a practice round. Once
we stopped trying to win every trade, the little alliances became much more
interesting.

> The best deal was the one that gave someone else a reason to help later.

Next time, leave the scoring cards face up for the first two rounds so new
players can see what they are working toward.
