A fictional two-player game that fits on a small table. Each card is either a
piece of harbor or a promise to a ship that has not arrived yet.

We lost the first game by making the harbor beautiful and forgetting that ships
need somewhere to turn. The second attempt was much less symmetrical and much
more useful.

Twenty-five minutes is about right, including the discussion afterward about
which decision started the trouble. Leave time for a second game.
