A fictional novel about noticing what a map leaves out: the bench that catches
the last sunlight, a path that only appears at low tide, a house everyone
remembers by a different name.

The chapters alternate between a journey and the notes for an unfinished atlas.
I preferred the small detours to the mystery that ties them together.

> A useful map tells you where you are. A good one gives you a reason to stay.

Worth returning to slowly, with a notebook nearby. The final chapter changes
the meaning of a very ordinary bridge from the opening pages.
