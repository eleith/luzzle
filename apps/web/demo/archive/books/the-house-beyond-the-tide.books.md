A fictional family saga built around a wonderfully impractical house. Every
visit begins with checking the tide tables; every departure leaves something
unfinished.

## What stayed with me

- The grandmother's habit of dating every repair in pencil beneath the stairs.
- A room whose purpose changes with each generation.
- The long argument about whether keeping a place is the same as preserving it.

It takes its time. At first the repeated crossings feel excessive, but eventually
the rhythm becomes the point: the same walk, a different person carrying the key.
