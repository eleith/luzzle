A fictional essay with a practical distinction: collecting an idea and finding
it again are two different jobs. A notebook should make both easy enough that
neither feels like maintenance.

I am borrowing three habits:

1. Leave a few blank lines after an interesting thought.
2. Give unfinished questions their own index entry.
3. Add a date when returning to an old page rather than rewriting its history.

The most useful example was a page with several crossed-out headings. You could
still see how the question changed, which made the final answer more convincing.
