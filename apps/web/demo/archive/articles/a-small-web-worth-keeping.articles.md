The part I keep returning to is the idea that a website can be a **workbench**,
not a finished exhibition. A reading list, an unfinished recipe and a small
collection of useful commands all belong there.

> Leave enough context that your future self can pick up the thread.

Things to try on my own site:

- Link related notes even when neither is finished.
- Keep the reason I saved something beside the link.
- Prefer a small page I can maintain over a large system I cannot.
